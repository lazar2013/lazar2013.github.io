Assignment 11 Summary

My biggest takeaway from the lesson materials and activities was learning about raster and vector images. A raster image is made from pixels, so it will get blurry if you stretch it out too much. I would use raster images for things like thumbnails, product images, and banners. Vector images are usually built from shapes and will stay sharp at any size. I would use those kinds of images for things like icons and logos. Additionally, I was able to explore adding audio and video files to my website, as well as embedding a YouTube video. These are useful skills to understand because they can be used to help create new training materials in the workplace, or just update internal pages to make them feel more alive.
