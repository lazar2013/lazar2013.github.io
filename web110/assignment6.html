In this session I learned more about different tools and methods to communicate virtually. In one of the activities, I went over and explored apps like WhatsApp and Zoom, which I have used before but never thought about how they work. I learned about VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) and how it converts your voice into digital packets that are transferable via the internet like emails and web pages. VoIP makes calling more accessible to anyone with an internet connection on apps such as Zoom, WhatsApp, and Google Meet for free. This forces phone companies to create more competitive pricing. My family uses the app Viber for connection with the rest of our relatives overseas. In the working world, tools like Microsoft Teams and Zoom are commonly used for work meetings, and communications amongst various departments within many businesses.

By working on the activity that focused on the File Transfer Protocol (FTP), I learned that it is basically about moving files between your computer and a server. I learned that regular FTP sends your username and password as plain text which means that anybody who is monitoring your network can read your username and password. For this reason it is safer to use options that encrypt this kind of information such as FTPS and SFTP. For this and a few other classes I've taken at Harper, we have used GitHub where I upload assignments and projects to a web server. I've also learned that GitHub is commonly used in the programming world, so having a little more background information on FTP is beneficial in the long term as well.

Through the activities, I also learned about Intellectual Property as well. This covered piracy and sharing copyrighted music, movies, games or software without permission. This is an illegal practice and people who do it can be sued or criminally charged. Many of these types of downloads may come with viruses or spyware as well. I also learned about peer-to-peer (P2P) sharing and how that is different from piracy and is legal. By doing peer-to-peer sharing people download files directly from each other's computers. This showed me the importance of properly citing the sources for the content you use for personal and professional online presence situations to avoid getting into any trouble. These activities showed me that the apps I use every day have technical and legal details that matter whenever I begin to build and publish things myself.
