After completing the readings and activities for the first week, I realized that there have been quite a few things happening around me that I have had some awareness of but not a full comprehension of what was actually going on. Starting off, in one of the activities I reviewed some background information about the internet and how it originally started in 1969 to facilitate government communication. At the time it was called ARPANET and only had four connected computers to it. By 1972, it became known as the internet. I also went over how computers actually locate each other through their IP addresses as well as different ways to physically connect to the internet. Having this foundational understanding and background information on the internet could be useful in my professional future by helping me troubleshoot programming issues more efficiently and having an overall better understanding of how information moves around physically through the internet. I also was able to look into my own IPv6 address and seeing how easily I can be tracked with geolocation tools. Both tools were able to accurately identify my internet provider, but they actually came up with two different cities for my location. I think I was most surprised to see that they can actually come up with a longitude and latitude coordinates for me though I think it’s just an estimate based on the assigned address for the ISP. This provided me with some knowledge into how privacy actually works online and will definitely be useful in the future as I’m sure that working in any sort of programming occupation, I will be handling data that is sensitive to some degree. Lastly, I enjoyed learning about and trying out the traceroute function from my own laptop to see the actual path my data takes to reach a website. The first several hops were routers within my internet provider’s local network and then it jumped to a national network until finally reaching the destination server. This was cool to see because this is happening all around us at all times and we aren’t aware of all the data that is traveling around us! Having this knowledge will definitely be helpful when trying to diagnose connection issues or just trying to understand why a server isn’t responding. Overall, I found the first lesson interesting because it had a lot of “AHA” moments for me. The materials felt simple to navigate through, and I like I discovered a world that has been around me but I never took notice of before. This makes me excited to learn more in the upcoming weeks.
